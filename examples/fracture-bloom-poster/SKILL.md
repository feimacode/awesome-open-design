# Fracture & Bloom — Exhibition Poster

1. Build a tall 1080×1920 poster canvas with a dark, cinematic backdrop and layered radial gradients in blue, violet, and warm coral tones.
2. Add subtle fracture-line vector details and bloom-like circular marks to give the poster a contemporary, gallery-ready texture without crowding the layout.
3. Use a strong editorial headline, with the main title staged in a large stacked format and a warm accent treatment for the ampersand and key words.
4. Place the exhibition metadata in a glass-like card with date, venue, and opening-reception details, then add a clean list of featured artists.
5. Finish with a restrained footer block that includes the curator name and a QR-style placeholder that maintains the visual identity of an art-gallery invitation.
