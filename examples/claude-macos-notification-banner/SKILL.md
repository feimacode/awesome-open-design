1. Establish a 16:9 video stage with a quiet, dark ambient background that keeps the notification legible.
2. Place a compact frosted-glass notification in the upper-right corner with a Claude app mark, timestamp, headline, body copy, and action.
3. Use Claude's warm parchment, ivory, near-black, olive, stone, terracotta, and coral tokens for the surface, typography, and accent hierarchy.
