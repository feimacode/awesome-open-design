Open Design Agent Kit · Workflow

Four steps from
prompt to published.

  1. 01

    Set up a design system

    Pick one of ~150 bundled systems, or import your own, and activate it in your agent.

    ◆ Design system: Neonactive
  2. 02

    Prompt the agent

    Tell it the content and the type of design: poster, deck, landing page, dashboard.

    > Make a 1080×1350 poster of our 4-step workflow

    type: poster content: 4 steps
  3. 03

    Review & re-prompt

    Preview it live, pin comments on any element, and loop until it's right.

    Publish or export
