A5 portrait (148 × 210 mm), single page, made to be printed on a home or library printer and pinned up, then photographed for a group chat or neighbourhood page.

- **Two inks only.** Deep green for line art, headline and labels; coral for the second headline word, seed shapes and one accent in the footer. Warm off-white paper. No gradients, no shadows inside the page, no photos.
- **Art band** at the top: flat line-art sprouts and seed shapes drawn as inline SVG with round caps, like a two-colour riso print. Keep it simple enough to read when printed small.
- **Headline:** one word per line, very large, tight leading (about 0.86), the second word in the accent ink.
- **One sentence** under the headline, in a warm, plain voice: what to bring and that everyone is welcome.
- **Info list:** label/value rows (When, Where, Bring) with monospace tracked labels and a strong rule above the first row. Labels in green, values in ink.
- **Footer:** who's hosting and how to ask a question, plus a small boxed stamp ("Swap don't shop") as the only decoration.

Use the user's real date, place and host. Where they haven't given one, write a clearly labelled `—` placeholder instead of inventing an address, a time or a group name. To adapt it to another event (a book swap, a repair café, a clothes swap), change the art to match the thing being swapped, keep the two-ink limit and the info list.

Export with the `a5` print format for a PDF with bleed, and the `ig-portrait` or `story` format if it will also be posted.
