A full-screen vertical Story (`story`, 1080 × 1920) for a stall at a market, craft fair or pop-up. Swap the drawing for the maker's own goods: candles, prints, plants, baked goods.

- **Awning:** a two-colour striped band with a scalloped edge across the very top. It sits in the area the app covers with its progress bar, so it's decoration only; nothing to read goes there.
- **Headline:** a large serif in three short lines, one word in italic in the glaze blue. A small tracked kicker above it says when ("This Saturday").
- **Drawing:** flat shapes of the goods standing on one dark shelf line, in the palette's three colours. No photos, no gradients.
- **Info panel:** a dark block with Where / When / Bring rows, pushed to the bottom of the readable area.
- **Sign-off:** what the maker sells, and their handle.
- **Safe area:** keep the top 250 px and bottom 340 px (full size) free of anything people need to read; Stories put the profile row at the top and the reply box at the bottom.

Use the maker's real market, stall and date; mark anything missing with `—` rather than inventing a place or time. For the same announcement as a square post, export `ig-square` from the same design and move the panel beside the drawing.
