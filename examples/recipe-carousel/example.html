Brown Butter Miso Cookies

Nutty, salty, chewy in the middle. Swipe for the full recipe →

Makes 1220 min + chillBake 10–12 min
2 / 3

You’ll need

  • Unsalted butter, browned 115 g
  • White miso 2 tbsp
  • Light brown sugar 150 g
  • Egg, room temperature 1
  • Plain flour 190 g
  • Baking soda ½ tsp
  • Dark chocolate, chopped 120 g

Brown the butter until it smells like toffee and the bits turn golden, then let it cool for 10 minutes.

3 / 3

Method

  1. Whisk the cooled butter, miso and sugar until glossy, then beat in the egg.
  2. Fold in the flour and baking soda, then the chocolate.
  3. Chill the dough for at least 1 hour.
  4. Scoop 12 balls onto a lined tray and bake at 180 °C for 10–12 minutes, until the edges are set.
  5. Rest on the tray for 5 minutes before moving.
