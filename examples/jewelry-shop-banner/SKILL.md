A shop banner at the `shop-banner` format (1920 × 800), the wide hero most store themes show at the top of the home page or a collection page.

- **Split layout:** copy on the left half on a sand background; a clay-coloured panel on the right holding an arch-shaped window for the product photo. The arch is the crop handmade sellers commonly use; keep it, and keep the photo inside it.
- **Type:** one serif display face for the collection name (one word set in italic, in the clay colour), a clean sans for everything else. A small tracked eyebrow line above names the season or launch.
- **One sentence** about the work: materials and that it's made by hand. No invented claims ("best-selling", "loved by 10,000 customers").
- **Button:** a dark pill with an arrow. Store themes usually overlay their own button, so keep it inside the left half and away from the edges.
- **Tag** in the bottom-right corner for the featured piece's name and price.
- Keep important text 120 px clear of the left and right edges at full size: store themes crop wide banners on narrow screens.

Use the seller's real product photo in the arch. Without one, keep the simple line drawing as a marked stand-in and say so. Write `—` for any name or price the seller hasn't given. For the same launch in other sizes, export `shop-product` (square) and `ig-portrait` from the same design.
